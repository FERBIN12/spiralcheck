How these numbers are computed

The app fits an ideal Archimedes spiral (r = a + b·θ) to your trace, measures how far each point sits from it along the radius, resamples that error to 100 Hz, removes slow drift, and finds the strongest rhythm between 3 and 15 Hz with a Welch spectrum. Amplitude is half the peak-to-peak size of that rhythm. Score = 100 · exp(−(4D + 8T + C)), where D and T are RMS deviation and tremor amplitude divided by the loop spacing and C is the variation in loop spacing. Distances are CSS pixels; mm values assume a nominal 96 px per inch and depend on your screen.
